# A Composer field

Phase 18's plan listed Composer among the fields worth suggesting, and `tracks`
has no such column.

It is a migration plus a scan change plus a `lofty` read plus a column definition,
`tag_values` entry and filter field — a small phase of its own rather than an
autocompletion detail, which is why it was left out.

Worth doing when somebody with classical music in their library asks.
